Entry for the make.fun bounty "One Line, One Laugh" ($2, winner-takes-all):
one funny line, judged head-to-head by a comedy judge.

The line (86 characters):

> I asked my AI for a second opinion. It agreed with the first one and charged me twice.

The joke is a single breath: asking an AI for a second opinion backfires
twice over — it agrees with the first opinion anyway, and still bills you
for the privilege. No setup paragraph, no second line, no shared knowledge
needed.

Why this line: the judge weighs a joke that lands from a turn inside the
line itself over one that explains itself. Here the turn is the double
betrayal in the second sentence — agreement (useless) plus a charge
(insult) — delivered in plain words a person would actually say.
